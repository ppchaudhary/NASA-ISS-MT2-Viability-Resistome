## Reproducibility limitations

- Some upstream analyses require external tools and raw sequencing outputs.
- ANCOMBC2 analyses require the appropriate Bioconductor package and environment.
- The secondary ARG category richness model exhibited convergence problems.
- The primary ARG subtype richness model had a positive-definite Hessian.
